# Product Overview — `aiossan.com`

## 1. Product Identity
`aiossan.com` is an interactive web reader and digital bookstore for the **Practical Leadership Playbook Trilogy** (*Trilogi Panduan Kepemimpinan Praktis*), designed with a **Swiss Editorial Publishing + Tactical SaaS** aesthetic (inspired by *Stripe Press*, *Harvard Business Review*, *Linear*, and *Refactoring UI*).

## 2. The 3 Books (45 Chapters Total — 15 Chapters per Book)
Every book has 15 chapters (`ch-0` Introduction, `ch-1` to `ch-13` Core Chapters, and `ch-14` Appendix & Primary Research Citations):
- **Book 1 (`book-1`):**
  - `id-kasual`: *Mahir Memimpin Tim: Strategi Bikin Tim Jadi Mandiri*
  - `id-standar`: *Mahir Memimpin Tim: Strategi Menciptakan Tim yang Mandiri*
  - `en`: *Practical Team Leadership: Developing Autonomous Teams*
  - **Core Frameworks:** 4x4 Leadership Framework (4 Core Conversations × 4 Communication Skills), 3K/3C Delegation Brief, L1–L4 Situational Readiness Calibration, 4-Week Coaching Simulation.
- **Book 2 (`book-2`):**
  - `id-kasual`: *Napas Panjang Memimpin Tim: Strategi Atasi Jenuh dan Bangun Sistem Kerja Otomatis*
  - `id-standar`: *Napas Panjang Memimpin Tim: Strategi Mengatasi Kejenuhan dan Membangun Sistem Kerja Mandiri*
  - `en`: *Sustaining Your Leadership: Practical Strategies to Beat Burnout and Build Self-Running Teams*
  - **Core Frameworks:** 3P/3R Weekly Calendar Audit, Anti-Monkey Management, 6 PM Psychological Detachment Protocol, Second-in-Command Bench Strength, 30/70 Job Crafting.
- **Book 3 (`book-3`):**
  - `id-kasual`: *Memimpin Para Leader: Strategi Naik Kelas dari Mengatur Tim ke Menggerakkan Divisi*
  - `id-standar`: *Memimpin Para Leader: Strategi Naik Kelas dari Mengarahkan Tim ke Menggerakkan Divisi*
  - `en`: *Leading Other Leaders: Practical Strategies for Moving from Team Lead to Manager of Managers*
  - **Core Frameworks:** 3S Cross-Team Shared Charter, 4P Manager-to-Leader 1-on-1 Audit, Anti-Skip-Level Guardrails, 15-Minute Silo Seam Mediation, 90-Day Divisional Blueprint.

## 3. Three Language Registers (`BookLang`)
Buyers receive instant access to all 3 language editions and can switch at any time with 1 click while staying on the exact same chapter (`ch-0` through `ch-14`):
- `id-kasual`: Conversational Indonesian (*Gue/Lo*) — tailored for startups, tech teams, and peer-to-peer reading.
- `id-standar`: Professional Indonesian (*Saya/Kamu*) — tailored for corporate, banking, BUMN, and formal enterprises.
- `en`: Global English (*You/I*) — tailored for multinational teams and expat managers.

## 5. Free Sample vs Paid Entitlements
- **Free Sample Chapters:** `ch-0` (Introduction) and `ch-1` (Chapter 1) of ALL 3 books in ALL 3 languages are 100% free and readable without login.
- **Locked Chapters:** `ch-2` through `ch-14` require an active row in `public.user_entitlements` (unlocked automatically via Mayar payment webhook and auto-claimed by email if purchased via Guest Checkout).
